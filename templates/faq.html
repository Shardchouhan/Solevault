How to Measure Your Foot Length in Centimeters (CM)
Step 1

Place a blank sheet of paper flat on the floor firmly against a wall.

Step 2

Stand on the paper with your heel lightly pressing against the wall.

Step 3

Mark your longest toe and measure with a ruler in CM to find your exact UK size.
