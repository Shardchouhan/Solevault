How to Measure Your Foot in Centimeters (CM):
  1. Place a blank sheet of paper on the floor against a flat wall.
  2. Stand with your heel resting firmly against the wall.
  3. Mark the longest point of your toes on the paper with a pencil.
  4. Measure the distance from the edge of the paper to the pencil mark with a ruler in centimeters (CM). Compare with the chart above!
