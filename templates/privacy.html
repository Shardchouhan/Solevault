PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SoleVault collects personal information provided when you create an account, place a sneaker order, subscribe to our private drop newsletter, or contact our concierge. This includes your full name, shipping and billing address, email address, telephone number, and shoe size preferences.

2. How We Protect & Use Your Data

We utilize enterprise-grade 256-bit encryption (SSL/TLS) for data transmission. Your personal information is utilized strictly to fulfill orders, conduct sneaker authentication guarantees, provide tracking updates, and deliver customized member drop alerts. We never sell or license your personal information to third-party data brokers.

3. Payment Information Security

Financial transactions and card details are processed directly through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways. SoleVault never stores raw credit or debit card numbers on our local databases.

4. Cookies and Session Storage

Our website uses essential session cookies to remember the contents of your shopping bag, maintain active logins, and preserve applied promotional voucher codes.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion of your account data, please write to privacy@solevault.com.
